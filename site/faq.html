Frequently asked questions

Everything about our eyewear, lens tech, and the ADS business opportunity.

What brands does ADS Sports Eyewear carry?

We carry premium sport and lifestyle brands including Oakley, Ray-Ban, Smith Optics, Nike, Polo, Wiley X, Serengeti, Rudy Project, Spy Optic, Bobster, Bollé, Kaenon, Switch and Rec Specs.

Polarized vs. photochromic — what's the difference?

Polarized lenses cut horizontal glare from water, snow and roads for sharper contrast — ideal for driving, fishing and golf. Photochromic lenses automatically darken and lighten as the light changes — ideal for cycling and running where conditions shift.

What are the best sunglasses for cycling?

Look for wraparound coverage, photochromic or Prizm™ Road lenses, no-slip nose pads and vented anti-fog frames. Oakley, Rudy Project and Smith are top picks for the peloton.

What about running and trail?

Go lightweight with grippy temple tips and photochromic lenses so you're covered from tree cover to open sun without swapping shades.

Do the sunglasses block UV rays?

Yes — every pair we carry blocks 100% of UVA and UVB rays, protecting your eyes from long-term sun damage.

What is your return policy?

Easy 30-day returns on unworn eyewear in original condition.
