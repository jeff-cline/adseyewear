Frequently asked questions

What brands does ADS Sports Eyewear carry?

We carry premium sport and lifestyle brands including Oakley, Ray-Ban, Smith Optics, Nike, Polo, Wiley X, Serengeti, Rudy Project, Spy Optic, Bobster, Bollé, Kaenon, Switch and Rec Specs.

Polarized vs. photochromic — what's the difference?

Polarized lenses cut horizontal glare from water, snow and roads for sharper contrast — ideal for driving, fishing and golf. Photochromic lenses automatically darken and lighten with the light, ideal for cycling and running where conditions shift.

Are these sunglasses good for cycling and running?

Yes. We specialize in performance eyewear with impact-rated lenses, no-slip nose pads, wraparound coverage and vented anti-fog designs from Oakley, Rudy Project, Smith and Wiley X built for cycling, running and multisport.

Do the sunglasses block UV rays?

Every pair blocks 100% of UVA and UVB rays, protecting your eyes from long-term sun damage on and off the field.
